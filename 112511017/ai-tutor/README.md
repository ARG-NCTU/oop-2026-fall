## Required format

Use the [course's AI Tutor for AOOP 2026 instructions](https://github.com/ARG-NCTU/oop-python-nycu/blob/d4ccf63b9252d0017e84d3bed10628f0f5ea7bc9/ai-tutor-2026.md).

Part A records five questions, answers revised after hints, a corrected misconception, a challenged question with its reason, and a remaining question. The tutoring cycle asks one question at a time and lets the student revise before the tutor explains.

Part B records the challenge, initial student approach, tutor help, revision, verification, and reflection. The tutor must not supply code, pseudocode, or a solution when generating the challenge. Personal answers and outcomes must come from the actual session.
