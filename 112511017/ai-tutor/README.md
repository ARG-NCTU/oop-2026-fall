## Required format

The response files use the sections from the [course's AI Tutor for AOOP 2026 instructions](https://github.com/ARG-NCTU/oop-python-nycu/blob/d4ccf63b9252d0017e84d3bed10628f0f5ea7bc9/ai-tutor-2026.md).

Part A records five questions and their answers, revisions after hints, a misconception with its correction, a challenged question with its reason, and a remaining question. The original interactive workflow asks one question at a time and lets the student revise before the tutor explains.

Part B records the challenge, approach, tutor help, revision, verification, and reflection. The original challenge-generation workflow asks the tutor to withhold a solution.

These written responses include AI-assisted answers, reflection drafts, implementations, and tests.
