## ② My Misconception
### Before: I thought…

Ternary Search (which checks two midpoints to divide the remaining search range into three equal parts) will make fewer total element comparisons in the worst case than Binary Search, because dividing the search space by 3 reduces the problem size faster than dividing it by 2.

### Now: I understand…

Even though Ternary Search shrinks the search window faster per iteration, paying for 2 comparisons at every single level makes it do more comparisons overall than Binary Search.
