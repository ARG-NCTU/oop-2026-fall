## ② My Misconception

### Before: I thought…

I did not have a major misconception that required revising an answer, but I tended to reason about efficiency by tracing the exact number of operations executed.

### Now: I understand…

Big O notation is not concerned with the exact runtime or exact operation count. It describes how runtime grows as the input becomes very large. Multiplicative constants, additive constants, and lower-order terms are ignored, while the dominant growth term is kept.

I also understand the distinction between sequential and nested work. Sequential sections use the law of addition, so `O(n) + O(n²)` becomes `O(n²)`. Nested work uses the law of multiplication, so an `O(n)` loop inside another `O(n)` loop produces `O(n²)`.
