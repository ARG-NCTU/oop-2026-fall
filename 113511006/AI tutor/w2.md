### Algorithm, Correctness, and Complexity

**Algorithm:** Initialize total and first_day to 0. For each day from 1 through N, add A + (day-1)*D. If total is at least T and first_day is still 0, record the day. Continue processing to compute the final total.

**Why it works:** After each iteration, total equals the sum through the current day. Days are processed in ascending order. The first_day guard allows only the earliest qualifying day to be recorded and prevents later qualifying days from overwriting it.

**Time and space complexity:** O(N) time and O(1) auxiliary space under fixed-size arithmetic assumptions.
